# Karigar Link v9 — Market & Buyer Match Intelligence

This release expands **only the Market & Buyer Match section**.

## Added
- Overall Opportunity Score /100
- Best market ranking with explainable reasons
- Demand, competition, price-fit and seasonality indicators
- Recommended selling channels
- Top 5 buyer priority ranking
- Buyer budget vs artisan price fit
- Buyer quantity vs artisan capacity fit
- Product, location and category fit indicators
- Expected initial order quantity
- Geographic opportunity view
- Historical product-specific sales signal when matching records exist
- Seasonal opportunity view
- Recommended next action: market, buyer, offer price, quantity and season
- Opportunity pipeline: Matched → Contacted → Response → Negotiating → Order → Completed
- What-If simulator for price and weekly production capacity
- Learning loop explanation: Recommendation → Action → Buyer Response → Order → Revenue
- Direct enquiry action from a matched buyer

## Design principle
Market Match answers **WHERE should I sell?**
Buyer Match answers **WHO should I contact?**
The combined module answers **WHAT should I do next?**

## Data note
Scores are local, explainable decision-support calculations. They are not guarantees of demand, price, buyer response, or orders.
