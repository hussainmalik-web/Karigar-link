# Karigar Link V2.2 — Product-First Intelligence

## What changed

The three V2 modules are now intentionally different:

### 1. Demand & Forecasting — PLAN PRODUCTION
Select one product. The module estimates near-term units, revenue, seasonality, capacity and production gap. It answers: **What should I make, when, and how much?**

### 2. Market Intelligence — CHOOSE THE MARKET
Select one product. The module ranks market types, explains demand/competition/price-fit/seasonality signals, and gives a next market move. It answers: **Where should I sell this product, and why?**

### 3. Digital Marketplace — TAKE THE ACTION
Select one product. The module turns it into a buyer-facing opportunity, shows relevant buyer requirements, creates enquiries and records marketplace actions. It answers: **Who can buy this, and what should I do next?**

## Real-world signal layer

The app checks availability of official sources such as GeM, Indiahandmade and the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts) website. It deliberately does not fabricate live prices, bids or demand figures. External information is labelled as an external source, while product calculations remain local and explainable.

## Free-first
No paid AI API is required. The intelligence engines use the local SQLite data, product attributes, historical sales and rule/statistical calculations.
