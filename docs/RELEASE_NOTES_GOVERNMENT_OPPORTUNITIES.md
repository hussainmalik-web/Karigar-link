# Government & Opportunity Upgrade

Added without changing the existing V2.3 intelligence modules:

- Government Schemes remains a separate support section.
- Added richer scheme metadata: support, target, eligibility, documents, application route, deadline guidance and verification date.
- Added filters for Artisan, SC/ST, Women, Weaver, Finance, Training and Marketing.
- Added National SC-ST Hub as an official support route.
- Business Opportunities remains a separate action section.
- Added current 2026 Government Handicrafts event records with dates, deadlines, location, stall count where published and eligibility guidance.
- Added filters for Gandhi Shilp Bazar, Dilli Haat, Digital Marketplace and Institutional routes.
- Kept official-source links on every record.
- Expired/closed opportunities are not presented as currently actionable unless clearly labelled as reference/closed.
- Existing products, forecasting, pricing, market intelligence, buyer matching, catalogue, historical data and impact modules are unchanged.
