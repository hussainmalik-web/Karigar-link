# Karigar Link — Product Scope v6

## Problem alignment
The SIH26090 brief calls for AI smart cataloguing, photo/voice input, buyer matching/marketplace linkage, branding, pricing and training support for marginalized artisans. Karigar Link keeps those requirements and expands them with historical-data intelligence, explainable recommendations and impact measurement.

## Core loop
Past data + product knowledge → clean/structure → understand → recommend → connect → record outcomes → learn.

## Why historical data matters
Existing sales records can reveal top products, markets, realized prices, seasonality and buyer patterns. The system should not require an artisan to start from zero. It should also work for new artisans with no history by clearly lowering confidence and using rule-based product/market signals.

## Data principles
1. Preserve raw uploads.
2. Record dataset lineage.
3. Show quality and limitations.
4. Never invent missing business facts.
5. Separate deterministic calculations from generative AI.
6. Do not claim causality from simple before/after comparisons.
