NEW COMMERCE DEMO (local only)
- Artisan Business OS: http://127.0.0.1:8765/

- Admin console: http://127.0.0.1:8765/app/admin.html

To test the full flow:
1. Open Artisan Business OS and choose Plans & Store Sync.
2. Activate the ₹199 plan using the clearly marked DEMO activation (no payment is collected).
3. For each product, set a stock quantity and optionally add an image URL/SKU, then click Publish / Sync.
4. Open the customer storefront, add a product to the bag and place a test Cash on Delivery order.
5. Open Admin and use the local development key `dev-admin-change-me` to review the order and change its status.
6. Customers can create an account or sign in on the storefront, and use Track order to check status with the order number and checkout email.

The customer storefront, subscriptions, inventory and order management are a local MVP. Real subscription billing, online payment processing, shipping integrations, full customer accounts, and production-grade admin authentication are not connected. Do not expose this build to the public internet as-is. Change KARIGAR_ADMIN_KEY and add proper authorization before deployment.
